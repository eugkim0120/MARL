BESS Market Sim Map

What has been built, what the battery's PPO policy sees each day, how the dashboard is put together, and a list of changes that would make the market more realistic, ranked by cost and expected effect.

1 · What runs today

Generators learn first among themselves. Their weights are then frozen, and each battery size is trained against the same frozen generators. Every effect is a paired difference against the same-seed market without a battery.

2 · What the battery's PPO sees, does and is paid

One environment step is one whole day. The battery reads a 27-number vector once, writes 48 numbers for all 24 hours, and gets a single summed reward after the 24 hourly clearings.

3 · The change you asked about: carry charge into the next day

Today each day is a separate episode. The terminal penalty exists only to stop the battery selling a free 50% every morning. Carrying SoC over removes the need for it, but the policy then has to see SoC.

4 · Visualisation layer

5 · Making the market more realistic

Ranked by what each one changes for a battery. The current market has one smooth demand wave, three generators, perfect demand foresight and no battery wear. All three make the market less like a real one.

Suggested order

Stage 1 · fix the battery model
  • SoC carry-over over 7-day episodes, SoC in obs
  • Degradation cost and 10–90% window
  • Weekly load shape
  • Panels: full-power hours, spread budget

Cheap. Makes the battery figures defensible.

Stage 2 · real spreads
  • Solar unit with weather draw
  • Steeper merit order, scarcity cap
  • Panels: scenario comparison, week strip

Changes the economics, not just the bookkeeping.

Stage 3 · strategic response
  • Arm B, adaptive generators
  • Several batteries
  • Forecast error and imbalance settlement
  • Panel: learned vs achievable

Most expensive. The long-run equilibrium question.
