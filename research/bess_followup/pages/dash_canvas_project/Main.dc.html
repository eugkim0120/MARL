Three questions, one switch

Every page answers one of three questions for the scenario selected at the top. Pages follow the framework: scenario ladder (rows of experiments), value channels (why a battery earns), and audience (policy maker or trader).

Framework the pages hang on
Scenario ladder S0 today S1 + SoC carry-over, wear cost, 10–90% window S2 + weekly load shape S3 + solar with weather draw S4 + adaptive generators Each rung re-pretrains generators and has its own no-battery baseline.
Value channels C1 within-day spread C2 between-day shift C3 scarcity peaks C4 uncertainty / imbalance C5 strategic response Only C1 is open in S0.
Reading rules Every effect is battery minus the same-seed baseline. Solid = 95% CI excludes zero; hatched = it does not. Units are relative only: % of the no-battery market, or yield per MW. A scenario that has not run is shown as dashed, never as zero.
