easy_marl · 3 RL generators + 1 RL battery · 20 battery sizes × 10 seeds

Can RL agents learn to trade power, and what do batteries do to the market?

Three generators learn to bid until their behaviour settles, then are frozen. A PPO battery is added and trained. Every effect compares the same seed with and without the battery. All values are relative, because the simulator's prices are made up.

A · Can RL agents learn the right trading behaviour? Mostly, with gaps
  • 200 of 200 batteries buy cheaper than they sell.
  • In 4 spot checks the battery earns 74–97% of the best schedule an optimiser finds.
  • Generators settle on prices about 2.1× what bidding at cost would give.
B · What do batteries do to the market? Small, and it moves money
  • Consumer bill falls by up to 2.8%; mean price by up to 1.7%.
  • Generator profit falls in every size, by up to 8.4%.
  • Gains and losses net to about zero (−0.2 to +0.2% of the bill).
